# SB Mobile Installations Post-Launch Growth Plan

**Document:** 27-post-launch-growth-plan.md  
**Project:** SB Mobile Installations Website and Installer Recruitment Project  
**Business:** SB Mobile Installations, LLC  
**Framework:** Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS  
**Hosting:** Cloudflare Pages static export  
**Primary objective:** Build a reliable flow of qualified installer candidates while developing sustainable organic and commercial visibility.  
**Status:** Working growth standard  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** September 10, 2026

This plan defines the work that follows launch. It turns the website into an operating recruitment and organic-growth asset rather than a static brochure.

## 2. Growth Principles

1. Business truth remains stricter than growth targets.
2. Active hiring demand controls job-page publication and promotion.
3. Evergreen recruiting content supports demand between openings.
4. Installer-network registration is measured separately from applications.
5. Commercial inquiries remain separate from candidate conversions.
6. Search visibility is earned through useful, accurate content.
7. Every recurring activity has an owner, metric, and review cadence.
8. No page, listing, or campaign is created only to increase URL count.
9. Conversion friction is reduced with evidence, clarity, and shorter paths.
10. Growth experiments are reversible and documented.

---

## 3. Primary Growth Outcomes

### Recruitment

- more qualified applicants for active openings;
- stronger visibility for mobile installer roles;
- a useful installer network between hiring cycles;
- better candidate understanding of requirements and assignments; and
- lower abandonment between job discovery and application.

### Organic and local discovery

- improved rankings for role, service, location, and question intent;
- stronger branded and nonbranded search visibility;
- accurate Google Business Profile and directory signals;
- citations and references in answer engines and LLM search; and
- expanding topical authority without thin duplicate pages.

### Commercial

- qualified service inquiries from approved audiences;
- clearer service and project-fit education;
- stronger trust through verified evidence; and
- measurable separation from recruitment traffic.

---

## 4. Measurement Framework

### Recruitment metrics

- active job page sessions;
- qualified application starts;
- application completion rate;
- application error rate;
- application source;
- installer-network registrations;
- referral submissions;
- recruiting-platform click-through;
- candidate FAQ engagement; and
- qualified applicant rate when operational data is available.

### Search metrics

- impressions and clicks;
- average position by intent cluster;
- indexed eligible URLs;
- rich-result eligibility;
- branded and nonbranded query mix;
- local visibility;
- crawl and indexing errors;
- referring domains or citations where relevant; and
- organic conversion rate.

### Commercial metrics

- commercial page sessions;
- inquiry starts and submissions;
- qualified inquiry rate;
- phone and email engagement;
- service and audience page performance; and
- commercial source and campaign mix.

No metric should require collecting unnecessary personal information.

## 6. First 30 Days

### Technical and search foundation

- [ ] Review crawl coverage and indexation.
- [ ] Fix broken links, redirect problems, metadata issues, and schema errors.
- [ ] Verify canonical and sitemap consistency.
- [ ] Review Core Web Vitals and mobile performance.
- [ ] Confirm image loading and static output.
- [ ] Review Search Console query and page data.
- [ ] Review Bing Webmaster crawl and search data.

### Recruitment

- [ ] Confirm every active opening has a current canonical page.
- [ ] Publish or update approved job-platform postings.
- [ ] Review application completion and error patterns.
- [ ] Verify the installer-network path.
- [ ] Collect candidate questions from recruiters and applicants.
- [ ] Update FAQs only with approved, recurring questions.

### Content

- [ ] Identify pages with impressions but weak clicks.
- [ ] Identify pages with clicks but weak conversion.
- [ ] Improve titles, introductions, CTAs, and internal links based on evidence.
- [ ] Publish only the highest-priority candidate or buyer resource.

---

## 7. Days 31–90

Priorities:

1. Improve pages supporting active recruitment markets.
2. Expand evergreen installer role and requirements content.
3. Strengthen internal links between careers, openings, process, requirements, FAQs, and network.
4. Build approved commercial service and audience pages where demand supports them.
5. Improve local entity consistency and approved business listings.
6. Test one conversion change at a time.
7. Document lessons from candidate and recruiter feedback.

Do not create location or service pages without distinct verified value.

---

## 8. Quarterly Growth Cycle

### Discover

Review search queries, candidate questions, job inventory, recruiting-platform data, GBP activity, conversion paths, and technical issues.

### Decide

Choose a small number of opportunities based on business value, evidence quality, search intent, and operational capacity.

### Build

Update existing pages first. Create new pages only when the content brief, source of truth, URL, schema, and conversion path are approved.

### Measure

Compare against the prior period and the relevant page or journey baseline. Separate seasonality and hiring-cycle changes from website effects.

### Record

Document changes, results, assumptions, and next actions in Git and the decision log.

## 9. Recruitment Content Program

### Core topics

- what mobile installers do;
- installer role expectations;
- required and preferred qualifications;
- tools, vehicle, travel, and documentation expectations when verified;
- how assignments work;
- application process;
- candidate FAQs;
- active hiring-market information;
- installer-network participation; and
- career development or onboarding only when approved.

### Editorial rules

- answer a real candidate question;
- cite or internally document the business source;
- avoid promising employment, income, assignments, or response times;
- link to the current opening or network path;
- update content when job policy changes; and
- retire content that becomes inaccurate.

---

## 10. Organic Search and Topical Authority

Build authority by connecting useful pages into clear topic clusters:

- careers hub to role, requirements, process, FAQs, and openings;
- role pages to relevant active jobs without implying availability;
- commercial service pages to industries, technologies, service areas, and resources when approved;
- articles to the canonical service or recruitment page; and
- every page to a clear next step.

Prioritize depth, evidence, freshness, and internal relationships over publishing volume.

---

## 11. Local Search Program

- keep approved business information consistent across the site and listings;
- maintain Google Business Profile categories, services, links, photos, and updates;
- use location pages only for genuine service or recruiting relevance;
- avoid virtual-office or doorway location claims;
- review local queries and GBP interactions monthly;
- maintain Bing and Apple business visibility where applicable;
- connect local pages to services and contact paths; and
- document location changes before updating structured data.

Recruiting-market pages and commercial service-area pages must not be treated as interchangeable.

---

## 12. Answer Engine and LLM Visibility

Support answer engines and LLM search with:

- concise answer blocks;
- clear definitions;
- direct candidate and buyer FAQs;
- visible source and review context when truthful;
- consistent entity naming;
- useful comparison tables;
- descriptive internal links;
- accessible, server-rendered content; and
- factual pages that can stand alone when quoted or summarized.

Do not write unnatural copy for a specific answer engine or claim inclusion that cannot be verified.

---

## 13. Conversion Optimization Program

Test:

- CTA label and placement;
- job-card information hierarchy;
- requirements visibility;
- application form length;
- network disclosure clarity;
- commercial form friction;
- confirmation next steps;
- mobile sticky actions when justified; and
- page-level proof placement.

Each test records hypothesis, audience, route, event, start date, decision rule, and result. Do not run several changes that make attribution impossible.

---

## 14. Recruitment Platform Distribution

For each active opening:

- create or update the canonical website job page;
- publish aligned platform listings;
- use consistent title, location, requirements, and status;
- link to the canonical application destination;
- use source attribution;
- monitor click-through and completion;
- close or update every copy of the posting when the job closes; and
- preserve an installer-network alternative where appropriate.

Distribution channels are governed by 29-recruitment-platform-distribution.md.

---

## 15. Candidate Feedback Loop

Collect structured operational feedback from:

- recruiting staff;
- hiring managers;
- installers;
- applicants;
- candidates who abandon;
- platform responses; and
- recurring support questions.

Classify feedback as content gap, trust concern, form friction, job-data issue, technical issue, or operational issue. Do not publish private feedback without permission.

---

## 16. Commercial Growth Program

After recruitment foundations are stable:

- improve approved service pages;
- add industry and technology pages where genuine expertise exists;
- publish useful buyer resources;
- strengthen local commercial intent;
- add approved proof and case studies;
- refine inquiry routing; and
- measure commercial conversion separately.

Commercial growth must not dilute the candidate path or turn the homepage into a generic lead-generation page.

---

## 17. Maintenance Cadence

### Weekly

- active job and status review;
- form and notification check;
- urgent Search Console or Cloudflare issue review;
- recruiting-platform status check; and
- critical content correction.

### Monthly

- organic performance review;
- local listing and GBP review;
- conversion funnel review;
- top candidate questions review;
- image and content freshness review; and
- technical performance review.

### Quarterly

- content cluster and URL review;
- page-pruning review;
- schema and entity review;
- design and component review;
- recruitment-platform performance review;
- accessibility and performance regression review; and
- next-quarter prioritization.

---

## 18. Prioritization Model

Score proposed work from 1 to 5 for:

- recruitment or commercial impact;
- confidence in the evidence;
- search or local opportunity;
- implementation effort, scored inversely;
- operational readiness;
- risk to business truth; and
- measurement clarity.

High-impact, high-confidence, low-risk work comes first. Large page expansions without verified content or operational capacity are deferred.

---

## 19. Content and Page Lifecycle

### Create

Create only after source, intent, URL, schema, CTA, and owner are approved.

### Improve

Update existing pages when evidence shows a content, search, or conversion opportunity.

### Consolidate

Merge overlapping pages and redirect the weaker URL when one canonical page serves the intent better.

### Retire

Remove or noindex content that is inaccurate, obsolete, thin, or no longer useful. Active-job closure follows the job lifecycle policy.

---

## 20. Reporting

Monthly reporting should include:

- work completed;
- active job and network performance;
- application and inquiry funnel;
- organic and local visibility;
- top pages and queries;
- technical or indexing issues;
- experiments and results;
- content published or updated;
- recruiting-platform activity; and
- next-month priorities.

Use plain language and distinguish observed data from interpretation.

---

## 21. Risk Controls

- Do not publish an unverified job or location.
- Do not create false urgency.
- Do not optimize for traffic at the expense of qualified candidates.
- Do not use personal data in public reports.
- Do not let stale caches show closed roles as active.
- Do not build pages solely for search variations.
- Do not automate publication without approval and validation.
- Do not allow commercial claims to drift beyond verified capability.

---

## 22. Definition of Done

Post-launch growth is operating when owners, metrics, cadences, and review processes exist; active jobs and network registrations are measured separately; search, local, answer-engine, and commercial programs have clear priorities; content and pages have a documented lifecycle; experiments are recorded; platform postings remain aligned; and each release preserves accuracy, accessibility, performance, and conversion quality.
