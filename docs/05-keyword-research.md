## 2. Research Standard and Limitations

### 2.1 Current research level

This is a qualitative keyword framework based on:

- the existing SB Mobile Installations website;
- existing recruiting language;
- the approved project strategy;
- audience-persona research;
- competitor website and careers research;
- current search-result language;
- current industry terminology; and
- authoritative job-search and structured-data guidance.

### 2.2 No invented volume or difficulty data

This document does not assign numerical search volume, keyword difficulty, cost-per-click, click-through rate, or traffic forecasts because no approved dataset has been supplied.

Quantitative fields must be added only from documented sources such as:

- Google Search Console;
- Bing Webmaster Tools;
- Google Ads Keyword Planner;
- Microsoft Advertising Keyword Planner;
- an approved SEO research platform;
- recruitment-platform reporting;
- website analytics;
- CRM source and outcome data; and
- internal site-search data.

### 2.3 Keyword status definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Priority | Directly supports a verified or high-priority project objective |
| Conditional | Valuable only when the related role, service, market, or fact is approved |
| Research | Requires SERP, volume, or audience validation |
| Supporting | Builds context and topical authority rather than direct conversion |
| Excluded | Misleading, irrelevant, unsupported, obsolete, or likely to attract the wrong audience |

### 2.4 Publication rule

A keyword is not permission to create a page. A publishable page also requires:

- verified business relevance;
- distinct user intent;
- useful and original content;
- an accountable owner;
- a valid conversion path;
- accurate claims; and
- appropriate publication and indexation approval.

## 3. Search Strategy Summary

### 3.1 Primary search objective

Earn relevant visibility for people seeking mobile installation work involving GPS, ELD, telematics, fleet technology, automotive electrical systems, and 12-volt vehicle electronics.

### 3.2 Secondary search objective

Earn relevant visibility for organizations seeking mobile installation, fleet telematics deployment, GPS, ELD, camera, tracking, and related field services.

### 3.3 Recommended approach

Prioritize search topics at the intersection of:

- audience;
- role or service;
- technical specialty;
- verified geography;
- engagement or project type;
- practical question;
- search intent; and
- conversion readiness.

Specific, accurate phrases are more strategically useful than high-level terms that are broad, ambiguous, or dominated by software vendors and job aggregators.

## 27. LLM and Generative Search Topic Statements

Pages should use explicit, supportable statements that answer entity and relationship questions.

Examples of the information architecture—not approved final claims—include:

- SB Mobile Installations is a mobile electronic equipment installation company.
- SB Mobile Installations recruits technicians with relevant GPS, ELD, telematics, 12-volt, and vehicle-electrical experience.
- Active installer opportunities are listed on the current openings page.
- The installer network is for future opportunities and does not guarantee work.
- Mobile installers may work at customer, fleet, yard, or shop locations depending on the assignment.
- Commercial service availability depends on equipment, vehicle type, geography, schedule, and project scope.

Each statement must be updated to match verified business facts before publication.

LLM readiness depends on clarity, consistency, evidence, crawlability, and entity accuracy. Repeating a phrase does not guarantee inclusion in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI experiences, Bing, or another answer system.

Final URL decisions belong in `08-url-strategy.md`.

## 29. Active Job Keyword Mapping

Each active job should have one primary title and one canonical page.

### Title construction

Use the real role title, such as:

- Mobile Installation Technician
- Fleet Installation Technician
- Telematics Installation Technician
- GPS and ELD Installation Technician
- Traveling Field Installation Technician
- 12-Volt Fleet Technology Installer

The final title must reflect the actual role. Do not add keyword variants, salary, dates, locations, company names, or promotional phrases to the structured-data `title` field.

Google’s current job documentation recommends concise, readable job titles and prohibits misleading, stuffed, or promotional titles. It also requires the detailed job information and `JobPosting` markup to appear on the specific job page. Source: [Google JobPosting documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting).

### Page-level keyword use

- One exact approved job title in the H1
- Location or territory in supporting copy
- Natural use of relevant equipment and skill terms
- Required and preferred qualifications
- Accurate engagement type
- Direct application action
- Date and status information
- Related role and careers links

## 32. Keyword Cannibalization Controls

### Common conflicts

- Careers hub versus jobs index
- General mobile installer page versus individual job pages
- Installer requirements versus training page
- Installer network versus active openings
- General telematics service versus equipment-specific service pages
- Service page versus service-area page
- Technology page versus service page
- Commercial resource versus service page

### Control method

For each URL define:

- primary audience;
- primary intent;
- primary topic;
- primary conversion;
- unique evidence;
- parent and child relationships;
- canonical status; and
- indexation status.

Merge or redirect pages when they do not provide meaningfully distinct value.

## 35. Measurement Framework

### Recruitment search metrics

- impressions by role, skill, and market cluster;
- clicks by careers page and job page;
- average search position as directional data;
- branded versus non-branded traffic;
- job-result eligibility and errors;
- application starts;
- completed applications;
- qualified applications;
- activated installers;
- source-to-activation conversion; and
- cost per qualified activated installer.

### Commercial search metrics

- impressions and clicks by service cluster;
- qualified organic visits;
- engagement with service and process pages;
- commercial inquiry starts;
- completed qualified inquiries;
- opportunity and revenue outcomes where connected; and
- assisted conversions.

### Answer and AI discovery metrics

- featured snippet visibility;
- People Also Ask visibility;
- rich-result eligibility;
- cited or referred visits where measurable;
- branded search growth;
- referral traffic from AI and answer platforms;
- server-log crawler patterns where appropriate; and
- conversions assisted by resource content.

Absence of measurable referral data does not prove absence of AI discovery. Use multiple indicators and avoid unsupported attribution claims.

## 37. Keyword Prioritization Formula

Use a documented scoring model rather than search volume alone.

### Suggested scoring factors

| Factor | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Business priority | 25% | Does the term support an active recruiting or commercial need? |
| Conversion intent | 20% | Is the searcher close to a qualified action? |
| Audience fit | 15% | Does the term attract the desired persona? |
| Operational truth | 15% | Can the company support the implied role, service, or market? |
| Demand | 10% | Is meaningful search interest documented? |
| Competitive feasibility | 10% | Can a useful page reasonably compete? |
| Content and proof readiness | 5% | Can the company publish something distinct and credible? |

Do not score a term highly when operational truth is zero. A high-volume keyword for an unsupported role or service is not an opportunity.
