## 1. Purpose

This document defines how active installer openings are prepared, published, measured, updated, and closed across external recruitment platforms and owned channels.

It works with:

The website is the canonical public source. External platforms distribute the opportunity; they do not override the approved job record.

---

## 2. Distribution Principles

- Publish only approved active openings.
- Keep title, location, work model, requirements, and status aligned.
- Use one canonical application destination.
- Separate active jobs from installer-network registration.
- Use platform-specific formatting without changing the factual meaning.
- Track source without exposing candidate data.
- Close every copy when the opening closes.
- Do not pay for reach before the application path is working.
- Do not publish to every platform automatically.
- Record owners, dates, and evidence for each channel.

## 3. Channel Categories

| Category | Examples | Primary role |
|---|---|---|
| Owned | Website, email list, social profiles | Canonical information and direct conversion |
| Job boards | Indeed, ZipRecruiter, comparable boards | Active candidate reach |
| Professional | LinkedIn Jobs, LinkedIn posts | Skilled and adjacent technical candidates |
| Search | Google for Jobs eligibility | Organic job discovery |
| Local workforce | Workforce boards, trade schools, associations | Market-specific reach |
| Referral | Installer and professional referrals | Trust-based candidate sourcing |
| Outreach | Approved direct communication | Targeted candidate contact |

Platform availability, pricing, policies, and eligibility must be verified before use.

## 4. Canonical Job Record

Before distribution, the job record must contain:

- approved public job title;
- status;
- canonical website URL;
- recruiting market or territory;
- location model;
- engagement classification when approved;
- responsibilities;
- required qualifications;
- preferred qualifications;
- tools, vehicle, travel, and documentation expectations;
- schedule information when approved;
- compensation information when approved or required;
- application destination;
- publication date;
- closing or review date;
- source attribution;
- internal owner; and
- last verified timestamp.

Missing consequential facts are a publication blocker or must be intentionally omitted with an approved explanation.

---

## 5. Platform Posting Record

Maintain one record per channel and opening:

- job ID;
- platform;
- platform posting ID or URL;
- title used;
- location used;
- posting status;
- publication date;
- paid or organic status;
- budget if applicable;
- canonical URL;
- source parameters;
- owner;
- last review date;
- next review date;
- closure date; and
- notes or policy exceptions.

Do not use an external platform record as the only job record.

---

## 6. Posting Workflow

1. Confirm the opening is active and approved.
2. Validate the canonical job page and application path.
3. Prepare the platform version from the canonical record.
4. Review title, location, classification, and requirements.
5. Add the canonical link and approved attribution.
6. Publish or schedule the posting.
7. Record platform URL, ID, date, owner, and source.
8. Test the public listing and application path.
9. Monitor performance and candidate quality.
10. Update or close the posting when the job changes.

No platform posting should precede the canonical page unless an approved operational exception is recorded.

---

## 7. Platform Copy Standard

### Title

Use a clear role title that candidates actually search for and that accurately reflects the approved job record. Avoid excessive punctuation, all caps, urgency, location stuffing, or compensation claims not supported by the record.

### Summary

The opening summary should state:

- what the installer does;
- the type of equipment or environment when verified;
- the relevant market or work model;
- the most important qualification;
- a practical expectation such as travel or documentation when verified; and
- the next application action.

### Responsibilities

Use concise bullets. Start with the work itself, not generic employer language.

### Requirements

Separate required qualifications from preferred qualifications. Do not add “ideal candidate” language that creates an accidental requirement.

### Call to action

Use one clear application path. Do not send candidates to a commercial inquiry form or an installer-network form for an active opening.

## 9. Indeed or Comparable Job Boards

Use general job boards when the role and work model fit their audience and the application flow can be monitored.

Checklist:

- [ ] Account and employer identity are accurate.
- [ ] Job title is canonical.
- [ ] Location and work model are accurate.
- [ ] Classification and pay fields are compliant and approved.
- [ ] Requirements are not inflated for search reach.
- [ ] Application destination is tested.
- [ ] Budget and duration are approved.
- [ ] Source attribution is recorded.
- [ ] Posting review date is scheduled.

Do not rely on an external “easy apply” flow if it bypasses required screening or privacy handling without approval.

---

## 10. LinkedIn Jobs and Organic LinkedIn

LinkedIn may support professional, technical, field-service, and referral-oriented recruiting.

Use:

- a clear title and market;
- a concise description of the technical work;
- approved requirements;
- canonical application path;
- company identity and contact context;
- employee or recruiter sharing only when approved; and
- separate tracking for the job listing and organic post.

Organic posts should not change the job facts or create false scarcity. Comments and messages should route candidates to the canonical page.

---

## 11. Google for Jobs and Organic Search

Google for Jobs eligibility depends on accurate visible content, valid JobPosting data, crawlability, and current status.

Requirements:

- active job page is accessible to crawlers;
- visible content matches structured data;
- title, location, employment type, and dates are accurate;
- application destination works;
- closed jobs are updated promptly;
- JobPosting is removed or changed when the opening closes; and
- the page is included in the sitemap only while eligible under the indexation policy.

Google for Jobs is a search-discovery outcome, not a substitute for platform distribution or a guarantee of traffic.

---

## 12. Local Workforce and Trade Channels

Potential channels include local workforce systems, trade schools, automotive and low-voltage communities, field-service organizations, and professional associations.

Before publishing:

- verify the channel's audience;
- confirm posting requirements;
- identify the channel owner;
- adapt copy without changing facts;
- provide the canonical link;
- document expiration or closure rules; and
- measure source where permitted.

Do not claim an affiliation or partnership merely because a posting appears in a directory.

---

## 13. Referral Distribution

Provide approved referrers with:

- canonical job URL;
- short role summary;
- market and work-model facts;
- key qualification;
- privacy-safe referral instructions;
- source or referral code when appropriate; and
- a reminder that referral does not guarantee hiring.

Do not give referrers private applicant status or ask them to transmit resumes through insecure channels.

---

## 14. Outreach Distribution

Targeted outreach requires an approved audience, sender, message, and compliance review.

An outreach message should include:

- why the opportunity may be relevant;
- the verified role and market;
- practical expectations;
- canonical job link;
- a clear way to decline further contact; and
- sender identity.

Do not scrape or purchase personal contact data without authorization. Do not misrepresent an active opening, compensation, flexibility, or hiring stage.

---

## 15. Social Recruiting

Social posts may support awareness and referrals.

Rules:

- use approved imagery;
- keep role facts aligned;
- link to the canonical page;
- use a distinct source parameter;
- avoid personal data in public comments;
- do not use “last chance” unless factually true and approved;
- update posts when an opening closes; and
- keep network interest separate from active application language.

---

## 16. Source Attribution

Use stable, allowlisted campaign parameters. A recommended structure is:

- source: platform or partner;
- medium: job-board, social, referral, outreach, workforce;
- campaign: job ID or approved campaign name;
- content: placement or creative variant.

Do not place names, email addresses, phone numbers, resumes, or free-text candidate information in URLs or analytics parameters.

---

## 17. Budget and Paid Promotion

Before paid promotion:

- canonical page and form have passed QA;
- platform listing is active and accurate;
- budget owner is identified;
- stop conditions are defined;
- target market is approved;
- source tracking works;
- candidate quality can be reviewed; and
- closure responsibility is assigned.

Stop or adjust spend when the application path fails, the listing is stale, candidate quality is poor, or the cost is not justified by evidence.

---

## 18. Monitoring

Review active postings at least weekly during a hiring cycle.

Monitor:

- listing status;
- views and clicks where available;
- application starts;
- completed applications;
- source;
- qualified applicant signal;
- cost;
- candidate questions;
- duplicate or spam submissions;
- platform policy warnings; and
- alignment with the canonical job page.

The strongest channel is the one producing qualified candidates, not necessarily the most clicks.

---

## 19. Platform Update Procedure

When a job changes:

1. update the central job record;
2. update the canonical website page;
3. validate metadata and JobPosting data;
4. update every external posting;
5. update social and referral copy;
6. verify application destinations;
7. record the change date and owner; and
8. confirm no stale version remains publicly active.

Material changes require a new QA review.

---

## 20. Job Closure Procedure

When a job closes:

- [ ] Update the central record.
- [ ] Remove or change the website Apply action.
- [ ] Remove JobPosting schema.
- [ ] Update visible website status.
- [ ] Close or update every job-board listing.
- [ ] Stop paid promotion.
- [ ] Update social posts and referral instructions.
- [ ] Redirect or provide a useful closed-job alternative when appropriate.
- [ ] Preserve an installer-network path when approved.
- [ ] Record closure date and evidence.
- [ ] Check cache and search visibility for stale active language.

Never leave a closed listing accepting applications without an explicit approved exception.

---

## 21. Platform Policy and Account Governance

Maintain:

- account owner;
- backup owner;
- recovery method;
- billing owner;
- employer identity;
- approved users;
- access review date;
- posting policy notes;
- suspension or appeal procedure; and
- data-retention expectations.

Do not share credentials in project files, chat, or public issue threads.

---

## 22. Candidate Privacy and Fairness

- Use approved, nondiscriminatory language.
- Distinguish required from preferred qualifications.
- Do not request protected or unnecessary personal information.
- Link to applicant privacy information where required.
- Restrict access to applications and resumes.
- Do not expose candidate data in reports, URLs, screenshots, or platform comments.
- Provide a method to correct or withdraw information where applicable.
- Obtain legal review for jurisdiction-specific requirements.

---

## 23. Distribution QA Checklist

- [ ] Approved active job record exists.
- [ ] Canonical website page is live and accurate.
- [ ] Application path is tested.
- [ ] Title, location, work model, and classification align.
- [ ] Requirements and responsibilities align.
- [ ] Compensation and schedule claims are approved.
- [ ] Platform account and employer identity are correct.
- [ ] Source attribution is configured.
- [ ] No candidate PII is in URLs or analytics.
- [ ] Platform copy does not confuse network interest with an application.
- [ ] Review date and owner are recorded.
- [ ] Closure procedure is known.

---

## 24. Channel Performance Review

For each channel, record:

- reach;
- click-through;
- application start rate;
- application completion rate;
- qualified applicant signal;
- cost or staff time;
- platform reliability;
- candidate feedback;
- policy risk; and
- next decision.

Retain, improve, pause, or remove channels based on evidence. Do not equate channel volume with recruiting value.

---

## 25. Definition of Done

Platform distribution is operational when each active opening has an approved canonical record and page, aligned platform postings, tested application routing, source attribution, an owner and review date, privacy-safe measurement, and a documented update and closure procedure. The website, structured data, job platforms, social posts, referrals, and outreach must not present conflicting job status or expectations.
